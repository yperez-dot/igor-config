# Naples hospital will drop two Medicare Advantage contracts in 2027

Naples Comprehensive Health told patients on August 26, 2026 that starting January 1, 2027, it will no longer accept Cigna Medicare Advantage plans or Wellcare Medicare Advantage plans.

NCH said people who use that hospital can still get care through Original Medicare, a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy, or a Medicare Advantage plan that stays in-network with NCH. Which of those fits someone is a personal decision. A licensed agent has to walk that with the client. We do not pick the plan.

## Why Annual Enrollment Period exists

This is exactly why [Annual Enrollment Period](/medicare-annual-enrollment-2027) exists. AEP runs October 15 through December 7, 2026 — and this year it matters more than most. Even if nothing else about your plan changed, a network change like this one can mean your hospital or doctor is suddenly out of network on January 1. The only way to know is to check now, before AEP, so you're not caught off guard when it opens.

This is a local network change, not a new CMS enrollment rule — but it's a reminder that plans change every year, whether or not you hear about it directly. AEP is your window to review, compare, and switch if you need to.

## If you or someone you help uses NCH

Check whether your 2027 plan still includes that hospital and your doctors. Confirm it in the 2027 provider directory when it's posted — not last year's list. NCH is posting 2027 in-network Medicare Advantage information at [nchmd.org/MAUpdate](https://nchmd.org/MAUpdate).

If you're comparing paths, start with how [Medicare Advantage](/medicare-advantage-miami) and [Medicare Supplement](/medicare-supplement-miami) treat hospital networks differently. Then check the actual 2027 list for NCH — not a 2026 card, not a memory of last year's doctors.
